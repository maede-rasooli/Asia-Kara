Return Policy

Clear, fair, and transparent return conditions for your peace of mind.

At Asia Kara Industrial Tools , customer satisfaction is our top priority. This Return Policy explains the conditions under which products may be returned or exchanged.

Eligibility for Returns

  • ● The product must be unused and in its original packaging
  • ● Return requests must be made within 7 days of delivery
  • ● Proof of purchase (invoice or receipt) is required
  • ● Items damaged due to misuse are not eligible for return

Non-Returnable Items

  • ● Consumables (electrodes, wires, filters)
  • ● Products with physical damage after delivery
  • ● Custom or special-order equipment

Return Process

  1. Contact our support team to request a return authorization
  2. Provide order details and reason for return
  3. Once approved, safely pack and return the product
  4. Our team will inspect the item upon arrival

Refunds & Exchanges

Approved returns may be exchanged for another product or refunded. Refunds are processed within 5–10 business days after inspection.

Need Help?

If you have any questions about returns or exchanges, our support team is ready to help.
